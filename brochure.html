How Software Connects to Equipment

The software remains the control center; physical equipment connects seamlessly where data translation adds clinical value.

The Integrated Health Ecosystem

By connecting software standards (HL7, DICOM, REST APIs) with clinical devices, your hospital eliminates manual transcription errors, accelerates diagnosis, and ensures 100% data traceability.

Imaging Modalities $\rightarrow$ RIS/PACS

CT, MRI, Digital X-Ray, Ultrasound, and C-Arm systems feed diagnostic images directly into PACS while pulling patient worklists from RIS.

Laboratory Analyzers $\rightarrow$ LIMS

Automated Hematology, Biochemistry, and Electrolyte analyzers transmit test results directly into LIMS and doctor EMR charts.

Patient Monitors $\rightarrow$ EMR

Vital signs from bedside monitors, pulse oximeters, and ICU ventilators can be streamed into digital nursing observation flowsheets.

Biomedical Devices $\rightarrow$ EMS

Every clinical device is logged in the EMS software database for routine PPM calibration, service logs, and maintenance alerts.
