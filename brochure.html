  • Ultrasound Diagnostic Systems: 3D/4D Color Doppler with cardiac, obstetric, and abdominal multi-frequency probes.

Reliable physiological life support instrumentation for Intensive Care Units and emergency wards.

  • ICU Mechanical Ventilators: Advanced invasive/non-invasive ventilation modes with integrated turbine drive.
  • Multi-Parameter Patient Monitors: 12.1" touch screens tracking ECG, SpO2, NIBP, Respiration, Temperature & EtCO2.
  • Syringe & Infusion Pumps: Micro-infusion volumetric pumps with anti-bolus safety alarms.

Automated clinical instrumentation with built-in digital interfaces for rapid throughput.

Surgical infrastructure designed for sterility, precision, and surgical team comfort.

  • LED Shadowless Operating Lights: Dual-arm surgical lighting with cold light technology and intensity memory.
  • Electro-Hydraulic Operating Tables: Multi-position surgical beds suitable for general, ortho, and neurosurgery.
  • Electrosurgical Units (Diathermy): Monopolar and bipolar cutting/coagulation with contact monitoring.
